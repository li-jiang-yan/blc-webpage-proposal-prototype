Our sanctuary is open for wedding bookings 🙂

Notes
  • The church premise is open for wedding use on Saturdays only.
  • It is closed for booking on weekdays, Sundays, Public Holidays, the month of December, and the season of Lent.

    For further clarifications, or to apply for a wedding date, please download and fill in the application form, and the list of attendees form closer to the date, and email it to us at blc_admin@lutheran.org.sg. We will get back to you shortly.

    Kindly note that bookings are also subject to restrictions put in place by the church.
